My name is Gabriel Murillo, and I've always been curious about how things work and how people connect. I enjoy learning new skills, whether it's through my studies in software development or simply exploring new ideas with friends and colleagues. I'm naturally introverted, but I adapt well socially and value teamwork because it allows me to learn from others and contribute meaningfully. Quito, Ecuador

I've called Quito home for nearly thirty years, and it's a city that never stops surprising me. Living high in the Andes means waking up to crisp mountain air and views of volcanoes that frame the skyline. The historic center, with its plazas and centuries-old churches, feels like walking through history, while the neighborhoods are full of warmth and everyday life. Over the years, I've come to appreciate the mix of tradition and modern energy — from local markets to new cafés. Quito is more than a capital; it's a place where culture, nature, and community blend into daily life.
